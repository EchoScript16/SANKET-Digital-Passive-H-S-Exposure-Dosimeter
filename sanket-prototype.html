Hardware Concept

The Badge, Worn Not Watched

A disposable wristband, no battery, no calibration upkeep. Two independent chemical reactions read at a glance or by phone: one for dose, one for shelf life.

01Progressive strip darkens permanently — position along the gradient encodes cumulative ppm·h, not a pass/fail line.
02Printed reference scale, light-fast pigment, sealed under the same window for in-frame calibration.
03Independent expiry patch — its own reaction confirms shelf life before the shift starts.
04Worker ID / QR for shift logging without manual entry.
A

Sensing chemistry

Indigenously formulated lead-acetate–free colorimetric layer, selected for a monotonic, near-continuous darkening response across the occupational range rather than a single threshold snap.

B

Sealed reference cell

The printed scale sits under the same clear window as the strip, so a single photograph captures both under identical ambient light — the basis for in-app colour correction.

C

Expiry patch

A second, separately encapsulated reaction that visibly shifts colour on its own fixed timeline, giving a pre-shift valid / expired check independent of H₂S exposure.

D

Form factor

Disposable, adhesive-free wristband, worn at collar or wrist height per DGMS placement guidance. No battery, no electronics, no field calibration.

STEP 01 · DIFFUSE

Diffusion

Ambient H₂S diffuses through a controlled membrane at a known, temperature-compensated rate onto the reactive layer.

STEP 02 · REACT

Reaction

The layer darkens irreversibly and cumulatively — each unit of ppm·h adds to the stain, it does not reset or fade.

STEP 03 · READ

Digital read

A phone photograph, corrected against the sealed reference scale, converts stain colour into an estimated cumulative dose.

What the app is compensating for

Lighting. Colour is read relative to the sealed reference scale in the same frame, not against an absolute colour standard.

Temperature & humidity. Reaction speed shifts with ambient conditions; on-device sensors (or manual entry) feed a compensation factor into the estimate.

Two independent reads. Dose strip and expiry patch are scanned and scored separately — one never masks the other.

Reading the result honestly

Range, not a single number. The confidence band reflects known non-linearity of colorimetric reactions at low concentration and long duration.

Position on the same gradient. The marker sits on the identical scale printed on the physical badge — what the officer sees by eye matches what the app reports.

Action thresholds are visible, not hidden inside a colour — OEL and action-level lines are marked directly on the scale.
